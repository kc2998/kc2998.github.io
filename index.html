Kevin W. Chen

MS in Data Science, Columbia University

I'm an MS student in Data Science at Columbia University who builds machine learning and statistical models for complex, high dimensional data. Previously, I spent three years at Columbia University Irving Medical Center in the De Jager Lab, where I analyzed single cell and single nucleus multi-omics to study Alzheimer's disease. That work contributed to three preprints and a book chapter on epigenetic mechanisms in brain development and neurodegeneration. My current coursework and projects focus on deep learning and scalable data pipelines, building on the analytical work I did in the lab.

Projects

BECCA

A Python and R package that estimates 7 brain cell type proportions from bulk DNA methylation. Trained on 446 donors with matched single nucleus labels, it outperforms four published methods on every cell type in held out testing.

GitHub → Quick start → Benchmark →

SiteRx NLP

Turning free text clinical notes into structured, searchable data, surfacing patient details that structured EHR fields miss.

Industry capstone · Ongoing · Code private

cAb-Rep

A public database of 306 curated human B cell receptor repertoires from 121 donors, with over 340M antibody sequences for studying antibody diversity, prevalence, and vaccine responses.
